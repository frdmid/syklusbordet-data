      managed at a Group level, together with other corporate activities. Corporate is not considered to be an operating 
      segment and includes activities that are not allocated to other operating segments.
      Transfer prices between segments are set on an arm’s length basis in a manner similar to transactions with third 
      parties. Where segment revenue, expenses and results include transactions between segments, those transactions 
      are eliminated on consolidation and are not considered material.
